Cycles renders of the teleop Wheel, v5. Use them for case studies and onboarding, never as UI imagery. For motion, use `model_rigged.glb` and the clips listed in the Hardware section.

- `wheel-v5-hero.png`: three-quarter front view, halo in Autonomy mint.
- `wheel-v5-hub.png`: the concentric faceplate: two key columns, E-stop, hazard, printed wordmark.
- `wheel-v5-junction.png`: the R5 fillet where the hub meets the rim, the micro-dot gum grip, and a key column.
- `wheel-v5-hinge.png`: the hinge barrel: fixed cap, 0.5 mm seam, knuckle.
- `wheel-v5-side.png`: side profile: base, barrel, column, tilt.
- `wheel-v5-rear.png`: the rear cover, paddles, barrel and USB-C.
- `wheel-v5-exploded.png`: part breaks along the wheel axis.
